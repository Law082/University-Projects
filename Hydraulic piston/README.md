# Hydraulic Actuation of Industrial Robots: Calculation & 3D Modeling

## Overview

This project covers the mathematical sizing and full 3D CAD modeling of a linear hydraulic cylinder designed to operate an industrial robot. The mechanism is engineered to safely move a 350 kg load on a $15^\circ$ inclined plane.

## Features & Results

- Component Sizing: Calculated the optimal piston diameter (50 mm) operating at 125.29 bar, and iteratively sized the cylinder rod (32 mm) using Euler's formula to prevent structural buckling
- Hydraulic Parameters: Determined the required pump power (1.054 kW) and flow rates for both advance and retraction strokes, confirming a laminar flow regime.
- Safety & Sealing: Verified kinetic energy limits during braking and selected appropriate standardized seals, guides, and wipers (e.g., Bosch Rexroth).
- 3D Modeling: Translated all theoretical calculations and selected standard components into a complete, ready-to-manufacture 3D digital prototype using CATIA.

## Technologies Used

- CAD: CATIA (3D geometric assembly and modeling).
- Concepts: Fluid mechanics, Euler's buckling formula, Reynolds number.
